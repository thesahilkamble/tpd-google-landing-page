How it works

1

Contact us

Reach out via WhatsApp or phone with your preferred dates and pet details.

2

Facility visit & temperament check

A mandatory 30–45 min visit with your pet to tour the facility, ask questions, and let us observe your pet's temperament.

3

Daycare trial stay

A mandatory trial day: drop off your pet in the morning and pick up in the evening so they get comfortable with the pack.

4

Confirm boarding & long stays

Once the trial is successfully completed, we confirm your pet's full-day boarding or extended stay.

5

Drop off & daily updates

Drop off during check-in windows (8–11 AM / 4–6 PM) and enjoy peace of mind with regular photo/video updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Pets Downtown cage-free?

Yes. Dogs are not kept in closed kennels. They stay within a gated compound where they can freely roam and have access to rooms, water and an open play area. They can sleep, play, rest or simply be themselves.

How much does boarding cost?

Dog boarding costs ₹900 per day.

How much does daycare cost?

Dog daycare costs ₹900 per day.

Are caretakers present at night?

Yes. Caretakers live at the facility and are present on-site around the clock. During the daytime, they actively supervise the dogs. At night, they sleep at the same facility in a separate room and remain present on-site, so they can respond if there is unusual noise or something requires attention.

What food is provided?

Chicken and rice is provided twice daily. Morning meal between 7–8 AM and evening meal between 6–7 PM.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Morning: 8 AM – 11 AM. Evening: 4 PM – 6 PM.

What vaccinations are required?

Pets should have their required vaccinations, including kennel cough and 9-in-1 or 11-in-1 vaccinations.

Do I need to give anti-tick medication before boarding?

Yes. Pet parents should give their pet an anti-tick medication or tablet before boarding.

Is there a mandatory trial before boarding?

Yes. We have a mandatory trial process to ensure every pet's comfort and safety. First, you visit the facility with your pet for 30–45 minutes so you can see the place, ask questions, and we can assess your pet's temperament. Next is a mandatory daycare trial where you drop off your pet in the morning and pick them up in the evening. Only after a successful trial do we accept pets for full-day boarding and longer stays.

Where is The Pets Downtown located?

The Pets Downtown is located at Wakad Gaothan, Old Bridge, near Wakad Bridge, Patil Nagar, Balewadi, Pune, Maharashtra 411045, India.

How do I check availability?

You can reach us via WhatsApp at 9604455664 or use the Check Availability form on this page to share your dates. We'll get back to you with availability.
